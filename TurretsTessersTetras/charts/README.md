# TurretsTessersTetras charts

Click a chart's title to open it at full size: click the picture to zoom in or out, and scroll to move around it.

Or **[open the chart viewer](https://wih4him.github.io/BEEmod-Beepacks/TurretsTessersTetras/charts/viewer.html)**: every chart for every item, one after another. Drag to move around, zoom with the wheel, a pinch or the buttons, and step with Prev and Next or the arrow keys.

Each item has a **box chart**: every box against every box, showing what the item does for each combination of its boxes and inputs.

- **Boxes by boxes, for a type**: the same kind of chart for one Cube Type or Button Type.
- **Types by boxes** (or **Timer by boxes**): every type, or every Timer value, against every box.

Each chart's notes and rules are at the bottom of the same picture.

Each chart was drawn for the pressing named in its title. Where an item changed later, its BEEmod description is the current word.
